# AccuPaid — Startup Plan (future company, not PayPal entry)

## Wedge to monopoly
Start: Hinglish payroll dispute resolver with approval gates for 50-500 person SMEs. Narrowest wedge is salary-day dispute triage, not full HRMS. Expand: vendor GST recon, then attendance-to-ledger close, then CA-partner API. Monopoly path is the calibrated dispute corpus per vertical plus approval audit trail auditors trust.

## 10-star version
HR forwards any Hinglish voice note or screenshot, AccuPaid normalizes, cites policy, proposes ledger fix, gets one-tap approval, commits, files GST-ready note, and logs LangSmith proof. 10 minutes becomes 60 seconds with zero unapproved moves.

## Business model
Pilot free 30 days up to 100 employees. Paid: INR 199 per HR seat per month plus INR 15 per vendor recon beyond 200 monthly. Annual prepay 10x monthly. CA-partner tier revenue share on notices avoided. Watch CAC payback under 3 months; kill if pilots need custom integrations over 2 days each.

## GTM first 100
10 pilots from founder network, 30 from Keka/Zoho partner directory outreach with teardown videos, 60 from CA firms referring GST-notice SMEs. No paid ads until 3 paid conversions. Demo asset is the 60s Hinglish approve-to-commit video plus eval table, not slides.

## Moat and copy risk
Omar lens: Keka/Zoho can clone lookup in one sprint. Uncopyable parts are the tail-eval harness with 48 Hinglish adversarial cases, the approval ledger auditors accept, and per-company jargon calibration. Ship those as the moat; keep RAG generic.

## Build phases
Phase 1 weekend: SQLite seed, 4 MCP tools, LangGraph interrupt, Qdrant 6 docs, 120-case harness. Phase 2 pilot: redaction, P95 latency gate, approval queue UI. Phase 3 startup: multi-tenant Postgres checkpointer, SSO, auditor export, CA dashboard.

## Kill triggers
No paid conversion after 3 time-saved proofs, Entity-WER stuck over 7 percent, approval fatigue over 40 percent reject rate, incumbent bundles free with distribution AccuPaid cannot match.
